How to play

Choose a bet using virtual cash and roll two six-sided dice. On the come-out roll, 7 or 11 wins; 2, 3, or 12 loses; and 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, or 10 establishes a point. After a point is established, roll that point again before a 7 to win. Roll a 7 first and you lose. Other totals mean roll again.

A winning bet pays 1:1: a $30 bet earns $30 profit if you collect it. When you win, tap GRAB THE CASH! before the timer runs out. If Phil gets it first, you do not collect the winnings. A losing bet is deducted from your virtual balance. The game may reset the starting balance when you begin again.
