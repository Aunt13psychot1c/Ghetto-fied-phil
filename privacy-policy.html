🔒 Ghetto-Fied Phil: Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Ghetto-Fied Phil is an entertainment dice game played with virtual cash. This policy describes information used by the browser game and what may change if Discord account features are enabled.

Information the browser game uses

During a game session, the game processes your virtual cash balance, selected bet, dice rolls, point number, timer, and whether you grab your winnings. These values are used to play the game. In the version reviewed, the browser game keeps its balance in memory and does not itself implement account registration, a persistent database, or analytics.

Discord interactions

If you access an integrated Discord app, Discord may send the app information required to respond to your actions, such as your user ID, interaction ID, command, or button selection. The deployed Discord integration's actual collection, retention, and deletion practices must be confirmed before enabling it. Do not assume that a Discord account is connected merely by visiting the game website.

Hosting and third parties

The site host, and Discord when you use Discord, may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs under their own policies. Game artwork and sound effects are delivered to your browser. The game operator does not sell game-session information.

Virtual money

All money in the game is fictional, has no real-world cash value, and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for prizes.

Storage, retention, and your choices

In the reviewed browser version, session balance is not stored permanently by the game itself, and reloading the page can reset progress. You can stop playing by closing the page. If saved profiles, leaderboards, logging, advertising, analytics, or persistent Discord account data are added, this policy must be updated with the information collected, why it is collected, retention periods, and deletion instructions.

Children

The game is not intended for anyone younger than the minimum age applicable to the platform through which they use it.
